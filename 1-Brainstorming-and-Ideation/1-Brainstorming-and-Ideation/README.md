# 1. Brainstorming & Ideation Phase

## Project Title
EduGenie – Google Gemini Powered Learning Assistant

## Introduction

EduGenie is an AI-powered learning assistant designed to help students understand educational topics in a simple and effective way.

## Problem Statement

Students often find it difficult to understand complex topics, revise lessons, and prepare for examinations using traditional learning methods. They need a learning assistant that can provide simple explanations, summaries, quizzes, and personalized learning support.

## Proposed Solution

EduGenie uses Artificial Intelligence to provide students with useful learning features such as:

- Topic explanations
- Summarization
- Quiz generation
- Learning paths
- Question generation
- AI-powered study assistance

## Main Idea

The main idea of EduGenie is to provide an interactive AI-based platform where students can enter a topic or learning material and receive personalized educational assistance.

## Target Users

- School students
- College students
- Learners preparing for examinations

## Expected Outcome

The project aims to make learning easier, more interactive, and personalized by using AI technology.
