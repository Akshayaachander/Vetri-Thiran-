1. Brainstorming & Ideation Phase

Project Title

EduGenie – Google Gemini Powered Learning Assistant

1. Introduction

EduGenie is an AI-powered learning assistant designed to help students understand educational topics in a simple, interactive, and effective way. The project uses Google Gemini to provide AI-based learning support.

2. Problem Identification

Students may face difficulties when learning new or complex topics. They may need additional explanations, quick summaries, practice questions, quizzes, and guidance for learning.

Traditional learning methods may not always provide personalized assistance to every student. Therefore, an AI-based learning assistant can help students receive learning support whenever required.

3. Brainstorming

The following ideas were discussed during the brainstorming stage:

* AI-based topic explanation
* Automatic content summarization
* Quiz generation
* Question and answer generation
* Personalized learning paths
* Simple explanations for difficult concepts
* Interactive learning assistance
* Examination preparation support

After discussing these ideas, an integrated AI learning assistant was selected as the project concept.

4. Proposed Solution

The proposed solution is EduGenie, a web-based AI learning assistant that uses Google Gemini to generate educational content.

Users can provide a topic or learning material, and the system can assist them by generating:

* Explanations
* Summaries
* Quizzes
* Questions and answers
* Learning paths
* Additional learning guidance

5. Objectives

1. To make learning easier and more interactive.
2. To provide simple explanations of educational topics.
3. To help students quickly summarize learning materials.
4. To provide automatically generated quizzes and questions.
5. To provide personalized learning guidance.
6. To use AI technology to support students in their learning process.

6. Target Users

* School students
* College students
* Students preparing for examinations
* Self-learners

7. Key Features

AI Explanation

Provides easy-to-understand explanations of difficult topics.

Summarization

Converts lengthy learning material into concise summaries.

Quiz Generation

Generates quizzes based on topics or learning materials.

Question Generation

Creates practice questions to help students prepare for examinations.

Learning Path

Provides a structured learning path based on the selected topic.

AI Learning Assistant

Allows students to use AI for additional learning support.

8. Expected Outcome

The expected outcome is a user-friendly AI learning platform that helps students understand concepts, revise learning materials, practice questions, and follow a structured learning path.

9. Conclusion

The Brainstorming & Ideation Phase established the basic concept of EduGenie. The project focuses on combining Artificial Intelligence and education to provide students with personalized and interactive learning assistance.
